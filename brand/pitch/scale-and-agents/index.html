Finding the right file

Everything lives in brand/pitch/scale-and-agents/, 1920×1080 at 30 fps. The name is built from four choices:

noura-clip[-bare|-titled][-alpha].mov|mp4   — square brackets are optional
clip
platform-loop · agentic-loop · scale-and-agents (the full arc)
chrome
-bare drawing only · omitted = wordmark only · -titled = wordmark and caption
ground
-alpha.mov transparent · .mp4 aurora baked in

For a slide that already has its own headline and background: noura-platform-loop-bare-alpha.mov

Putting it in the deck

Transparent versions

The .mov files carry a real alpha channel (HEVC with alpha) — Keynote and Safari composite them straight onto your slide. PowerPoint and Google Slides ignore video alpha; there, use the MP4s on a dark slide.

Only the marks are opaque; 92–97 % of every frame is empty.

Three cuts of each clip

-bare is the drawing alone: no caption, no wordmark, nothing but the geometry. Plain adds the wordmark; -titled adds the caption too.

Toggle Captions and Wordmark above to see each.

They need a dark slide

The marks are white, so on a light background they disappear. Try the Light slide preview above — that is exactly what would happen. On light decks, keep the baked-in aurora version instead.
